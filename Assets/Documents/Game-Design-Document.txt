Game Design Document: Casual 3D Mafia
1. Overview
Genre: Casual 3D Social Deduction
Platform: Mobile (iOS/Android)
Target Audience: 12+, casual players (quick sessions)
Match Duration: 8-12 minutes
Players: 6-12 (online multiplayer, auto-matchmaking)
Core Loop: Day (discussion + vote) → Night (actions) → Repeat
Win Conditions:
◦ Mafia: Eliminate enough civilians (majority dead)
◦ Civilians: Eliminate all Mafia
Key Features: No chat, card-based discussion, auto-host, AFK auto-actions, 3D room
